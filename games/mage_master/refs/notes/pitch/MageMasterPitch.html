Mage Master Piç Pitch

Summary: A fully idle auto-battler RPG where you never touch the fight. You summon gear from a rift, build three mages around it, and push deeper Levels. The whole game is the meta between runs.

 1. Hook

You are the master, never the fighter. Pull weapons and armor from a rift that reaches across ten ages of power, from a humble Uncommon to an Ultimate rarity, hand them to your three mages, and watch them auto-clear the arena. Every decision you make happens between battles: what to summon, what to equip, what to melt for gold, and how far to upgrade the Rift.

2. Pillars

  1. Gear is the build. Mages never level and never change. One hundred percent of a mage's power and playstyle comes from its two equipped items. Loot is progression.
  2. The meta is the game. The battle runs itself. Every real decision you make happens between fights: what to summon, what to equip, what to melt for gold, and how far to push the Rift.
  3. Every weapon is a playstyle. A weapon's four independent traits (Range, Pattern, Element, Stats) fully define how a mage fights, so two mages with different weapons play like different characters.

4. Look & Feel

Portrait mobile, top-down camera. Your three mages hold a camp ledge along the bottom; enemies spawn at the top and advance downward. A vertical stage track on the left edge shows how far through the Level's stages you are.

Battle screen:  The left edge shows the stage track; the bottom HUD gives each mage a name, HP bar, and two gear slots (weapon + armor).

5. Core Loop

One run: spend Energy, enter a Level, clear Stages, the Level completes, the next Level unlocks. Between runs you summon, equip or discard, and upgrade the Rift. At the end of every stage mages run forward to the new stage starting position and the camera follows. The whole engine is one feedback cycle: clears fund summons, summons feed power, power funds deeper clears.

What ends a stage: all enemies in the stage are defeated. What ends a run: clearing all stages (win) or all three mages dying (loss, back to menu).

6. The Three Mages

The party is fixed: exactly three mages, forever. Art is mage-styled, but the archetypes are Tank, Warrior, Support. Mages do not level up. All power equals class base stats plus equipped gear. The three form an interdependent cycle: the Tank buys time, the Warrior spends it, the Support refills it.

Each mage has exactly two slots: Weapon and Armor.

9. The Summon Rift (Gacha)

Spend Summon Crystals to pull weapons and armor from the Summon Rift. The Rift is the second idle clock: you spend Gold to upgrade it, wait out a real-time timer, and the upgrade permanently improves your pull odds. Better odds pull rarer gear, rarer gear clears deeper Levels, deeper Levels pay more Gold and Crystals.

Ten rarity ages (low to high), original to Mage master:

Common→ Uncommon→ Rare→ Epic→ Legendary→ Mythic→ Immortal→ Astral→ Celestial → Ultimate

Rarity does two jobs:
- Magnitude. Higher age means dramatically stronger primary stats.
- Stat count. Higher age means the item carries more stats. Low ages roll the primary stat only; high ages stack several substats on top, which is the late-game chase layer.

Upgrading the Rift: the Rift is upgraded with Gold, and each upgrade takes real time (the second idle clock). A higher Rift level reshapes pull odds so probability slides toward rarer ages and the lowest ages drop off entirely. This is the same shape as a classic idle summon-level odds table. In-game, real odds are always shown.

Use or Discard (per pull):
- Use. Equip it immediately; it swaps with the currently equipped item in that slot (the old item is converted to gold).
- Discard. Convert the item to Gold.
- No merging or fusing.

11. Progression

For v1 the meta is simply passing Levels. Beat Levels, get better gear (drops plus a better Rift), push deeper, raise offline income, repeat. That is the whole ladder.

  • Each Level x number of Stages; clearing all unlocks the next Level.
  • Difficulty ramps per Level, spiking at the last stage boss-type encounter. Player gear power should lead difficulty slightly early (the hook), then converge (the mastery test).
  • No prestige or ascension in v1, noted as a future layer once the Level ladder's length is validated.
  • No "Timeline" terminology; progression is simply Levels.

12. Audience & Platform

  • Who: idle and auto-battler fans, and gacha-collection players who enjoy the pull-and-build loop without twitch execution.
  • Platform: mobile, portrait. Top-down camera, party of three at a camp ledge, enemies advancing from the top.
  • Session: short active bursts several times a day, plus offline accrual
