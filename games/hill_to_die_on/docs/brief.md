# This is my hill to die on — design brief

Game id: `hill_to_die_on`

A lone holdout defends a small hill against escalating crowds. The player cannot retreat: their decisions are where to aim, which upgrades to draft between waves, and how to arrange a compact permanent stronghold. Failed attempts earn resources for the next stand.

Feel: pressured, readable, deliberate, escalating. The silhouette of the hill remains recognizable under heavy crowds. Warm ammunition and hit flashes contrast with muted olive terrain. Fire is a broad cone, lightning a chain, mortar a purple blast, and the rivet gun a fast piercing stream.

Portrait iOS first. A square battlefield sits above a floating thumb joystick; no input is needed to keep firing. Frame time takes priority over visual complexity. No mazes, physics simulation, movement stats, or sprawling construction in this slice. Upgrades happen at wave boundaries so combat stays uninterrupted.

The [vertical-slice contract](vertical-slice.md) records the current scope, balance assumptions, and architecture. Numbers are deliberately editable; this is a prototype for playing and revising the loop.
