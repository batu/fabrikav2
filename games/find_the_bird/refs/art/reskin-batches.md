# Find the Bird asset-generation batches

The 40 non-level reskin slots are divided by screen and visual responsibility. Each batch is reviewed and approved independently before its assets replace runtime files.

## Fixed art direction

- P3 Bold Cardboard
- P3 Explorer Bluebird mascot
- Flat-shaded cardstock, crisp cut edges, controlled cardboard depth
- Calm surfaces with restrained paper tooth
- Stylized storybook birds with exactly two wings and two legs
- Tools may use folded wings or straps; never human arms, hands, or fingers

## Per-batch workflow

1. Generate one concept sheet covering every slot in the batch.
2. Review the sheet in Portal and record the decision.
3. Produce each approved runtime asset independently on a perfectly flat `#FF00FF` chroma-key background. Do not crop runtime assets from the concept sheet.
4. Remove the chroma key with the image-generation skill's `remove_chroma_key.py` helper using border sampling, soft matte, and despill.
5. Validate every PNG before ingest: RGBA color type, transparent corners, non-empty visible bounds, no magenta contamination, no pale fringe on light or dark backgrounds, and sufficient transparent padding for its runtime slot.
6. Downscale only after alpha cleanup with an alpha-aware resampler, then repeat the alpha checks at final dimensions.
7. Apply approved files through the design-sheets round trip; do not drop generated bytes directly into the generated `design/assets/` directory.
8. Confirm the mapped `public/ui/` copies, run design-sheet validation, asset parity tests, typecheck, and the focused visual smoke check.

## Transparency acceptance

- Icons and character cutouts require real transparent pixels; a cream, white, or checkerboard-looking baked background is a failure.
- Background and pattern assets declare their intended opacity explicitly and are not forced through cutout processing.
- Edge validation is performed over both `#FFFDF4` and `#20242A` test backgrounds to expose light halos and premultiplied-alpha contamination.
- If chroma removal damages an asset or leaves a fringe after one corrected pass, stop that asset and use the image-generation skill's true-transparency fallback only after explicit approval.
