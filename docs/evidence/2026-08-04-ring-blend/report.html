Bird blending — A: old magenta paint-in · B: current flat-key · C: ring-marker experiment

C = the scene crop is sent with only a magenta ring marking the target, so the model sees the scene and paints the bird in place — contact shadows, occlusion, matching light — then we recover the cutout by diffing against the clean crop, bounded by the ring. 6 birds, pirate cove, Gemini Flash, one attempt each, ~$0.10 total.

Result: 6/6 painted blended birds with contact shadows. 4/6 kept the rest of the crop pixel-stable (<10% drift); 2/6 re-rendered the whole crop subtly (60% drift) — harmless in production because we paste back only the ring interior and discard everything outside it. The recovered cutouts include the shadow, which the flyout sprite can drop or keep.

My read

The ring approach recovers the blending we lost without giving up deterministic cutout recovery. The engineering needed to productionize: ring-bounded paste-back with a feathered edge, a drift gate (reject >N% change inside-ring-but-not-bird), and shadow/sprite separation for the flyout. Worth a full-level pilot before rolling into the 10-level run.
