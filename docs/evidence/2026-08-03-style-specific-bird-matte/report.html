Fine edges cannot rescue the wrong bird mask.

A style-specific ViTMatte decoder learned the synthetic task, then failed the real one. It passed 1 of 20 locked cases—well behind the existing SAM2-native control at 8 of 20.

Do not advance Codex recommendation only. Ticket 27 is awaiting Batu’s Portal verdict.

Answer

The fine-tuned matte does not solve clean bird extraction. It reduces scene pixels on a few cases, but still loses complete birds, hollows interiors, and keeps market, tent, fence, water, and shoreline fragments. Synthetic validation quality did not transfer to the locked real cohort.

Implementation audit

The host lacked the CUDA toolkit required to compile FastGeodis. SAMRefiner fixes its distance parameter at λ=0, where the generalized distance reduces exactly to Euclidean distance, so the runner used SciPy’s CPU EDT without changing the method’s distance field.
