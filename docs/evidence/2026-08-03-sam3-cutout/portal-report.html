Decision-ready route

What can run next

1 · SAM3 image zero-shot

Live. Soft probability edge available; topology and fringe fail the bar.

2 · SAM3.1 one-frame

Live. Hard masks only; nearly identical topology and harsher edge.

3 · Mask-head adaptation

Freeze detector and backbone; train the segmentation head on 20k illustration composites.

4 · Interactive decoder

Train point/box prompting on 50k perturbed hitbox views. First choice after this result.

5 · SAM3 + ViTMatte-S

Train exact soft alpha inside a SAM-derived trimap after topology is complete.

6 · BoundaryNet

Cheap 1–3M parameter residual model for ±24 px edge repair and future scale.
