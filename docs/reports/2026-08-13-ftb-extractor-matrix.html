Recommendation

OpenAI beats the Gemini default decisively in this run, and 2×2 beats 1×1 on quality-per-dollar. OpenAI completed 4/4 levels with 60/60 accepted sprites; Gemini completed 0/4 because every measured flatkey check failed and the free-chain fallback left incomplete birds. Within OpenAI, 2×2 preserved the same 100% pickupUsable and gate-pass rates while costing 69% less per accepted sprite and producing a lower mean regeneration-review probability than 1×1. Keep defaults unchanged as requested; treat the Gemini result as a reproducible regression to investigate, not as authority to change production.

Method & accounting

Default recipe canonical-magenta-v1, hash sha256:c09c136734afa783c92fe8a76b59777c44b34c1f3184df5230465e2c37942463. OpenAI flatkey model was selected from the live provider catalog as openai/gpt-5.4-image-2; Gemini control used google/gemini-2.5-flash-image. The report’s $14.530629 matrix total is the sum of before/after ledger deltas for the eight listed attempts. The $16.670794 all-in total adds $2.140164 of probes and excluded interrupted baselines. Unknown-cost direct-OpenAI/Fal rows were not estimated. “Accepted” means the level reached the final cutout blessing; fallback sprites in failed levels are shown but not counted accepted. Regeneration probability is the repository’s review-ranking model, not an automatic regenerate decision.
