Cats & Soup (Hidea / NEOWIZ) as the reference

Official App Store screenshots, plus the fandom wiki for mechanics. Sources and raw notes in catsoup/findings.md. Where the wiki did not confirm a detail it is marked unverified there.

What the screenshots show

  • One continuous vertical scene, top-down painterly meadow. Every station is a round plot on a path with a small Lv flag. No panels, no cards on the main screen.
  • Cats animate at their station with onomatopoeia text (THUD, WHIZZ, FRACK). The character on the plot is the reward, and the animation is the life of the screen.
  • One collect tray at the bottom: dishes accumulate on a wooden strip with their gold value; tap to collect. Not a sack per station.
  • Unbuilt plots are dimmed in place with the requirement flag visible, so the next purchase is always on screen.
  • Top HUD: currencies. Left: player level badge and shortcuts. Right: shop, rooms, camera.

Mechanics from the wiki

  • Cats have stars (rarity) and a heart level (affection, raised by feeding fish); both multiply the station's output.
  • Gear is four slots (hat, clothes, accessory, friend), each levelled 1 to 10 additively via chest rolls (ad or gems). Not a costume closet.
  • Owning every cat in a collection group gives a permanent production bonus to a named facility.
  • Facilities unlock in a linear chain ("unlocked after X"), one system at a time.
  • Offline gold has a base cap; an IAP extends it by 3 hours.

Changes to our screens

  1. Sanctuary loses the per-house coin sack; a bottom coin tray collects everything with one tap.
  2. Tier is a flag on the branch, the Upgrade button lives in the house's tap sheet, not on the scene.
  3. Signposts show the requirement, not "coming soon".
  4. Birds must animate on the pedestal from day one (tweens), with the sprite-sheet reaction as the Mastered reward.
  5. Collection page keeps the cabinet; Cats & Soup has no confirmed collection grid, so ours stays as designed, plus a set-completion bonus line on the shelf plaque.
