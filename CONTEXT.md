# Find the Bird Authoring

## Language

**Hitbox review**: Human confirmation that the current hitbox geometry correctly identifies every painted bird. Any geometry change makes this review stale. Cutout work starts only after this review.

_Avoid_: HB, hitbox blessing, “bless level”

**Cutout review**: Human confirmation that the current pickup cutouts and their sprite placements are good. It follows hitbox review and becomes stale when hitboxes, cutout pixels, placement, or flips change.

_Avoid_: CUT, final cutout blessing, golden blessing, “bless level”
