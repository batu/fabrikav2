What moves difficulty

Size alone

−0.4 dogs

T2's one-notch smaller dogs barely moved the matched mean. Compact scale is useful as a supporting constraint, not a standalone lever.

Moderate half-occlusion

−2.8 dogs

T3 is the only material matched drop. Heads remain visible, so levels stay fair while partial silhouettes and prop overlap defeat localization more often.

All-dog occlusion

−0.2 dogs

T4 recovered almost to baseline. Broad “every dog” hiding language is less consistently obeyed than a mixed visible/hidden composition.

Deep hiding

+1.2 dogs

T5 increased localization. Head/tail/paws-only language produced conspicuous fragments and extra target-like marks; hiding outran prompt compliance, not detection.

Far map

+3.2 dogs

T6's wider map created more usable pockets and more dogs. Zooming out raised capacity more reliably than it raised hiddenness.

Tone matching

24.2 mean

T9 did not show a lower count on independent scenes. Coat-to-prop color matching raised extraction effort, but scene archetype dominated the observed mean.

Interpretation boundary

T1–T6 are useful controlled evidence, but they preserve an execution mistake. The same five scene archetypes were reused across tiers. That supports matched comparisons, yet it was not Batu's intended “five unrelated generations per batch” design.

T7–T10 correct the design. Each tier uses five unrelated setting families with no scene reuse. Those tiers are better diversity probes but their means are confounded by scene archetype, so they cannot by themselves prove a prompt delta.

The metric is localization capacity. Counts show how many playable targets survived generation and detection. Eyes-on overlay review, full-resolution zoom, residue checks, sprite review, canonical state, and integrity audits establish playability; they do not substitute for measured human solve time.

T10 supplies the required confirmation. Its five unrelated scenes carry T1's exact view and T3's exact dog delta. The mean fell to 16.2, confirming the direction across new settings. Because scene identity also changed, T10 cannot establish the exact causal magnitude.

Cutout quality is part of the result. Tight default crops clipped six fully visible dogs in T10; explicit stable-ID scene boxes restored them before approval. Prompt-driven partial bodies were accepted only when the scene itself occluded the dog.
